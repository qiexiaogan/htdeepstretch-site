What is DeepStretch+

A deeper stretch, built around you.

DeepStretch+ is personalized, one-on-one assisted stretching designed to help you move with greater freedom and less discomfort. Beyond the stretch itself, our goal is to help you build better mobility habits so you can stay active, move well, and live healthier.

01 — Personalized

Stretching designed for you.

Every session is tailored to your body, mobility needs, and goals.

02 — Deeper

Feel the difference of assisted stretching.

With guided assistance, you can move into a deeper stretch and reach areas that can be difficult to target effectively on your own.

03 — Purposeful

Every stretch has a purpose.

Each session builds on your personalized stretch plan, helping you make consistent progress and experience the long-term benefits of better mobility.

04 — Private

Your space. Your session.

Every session takes place in a private, dedicated space—so the focus stays entirely on you.

Meet your stretch therapist

Haitao Wu

Founder · Stretch Therapist · Exercise Physiologist

Haitao has a master’s degree in Kinesiology and has always believed in staying active. Outside the studio, you’ll often find him playing pickleball or badminton, strength training, or climbing.

His own love of movement shapes the way he approaches stretching. He founded DeepStretch+ around a simple belief: every body is different, and stretching should be too.

Through personalized stretch programs, his goal is to help each client move more freely, stay active, and keep doing the things they love.
