Meet your stretch therapist

Haitao Wu

Founder · Stretch Therapist · Exercise Physiologist

Haitao has a master’s degree in Kinesiology and has always believed in staying active. Outside the studio, you’ll often find him playing pickleball or badminton, strength training, or climbing.

His own love of movement shapes the way he approaches stretching. He founded DeepStretch+ around a simple belief: every body is different, and stretching should be too.

Through personalized stretch programs, his goal is to help each client move more freely, stay active, and keep doing the things they love.
